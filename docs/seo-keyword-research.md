# SEO keyword research: CodexMarathon

**Research dates:** 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-15

**Source:** Ubersuggest MCP, authenticated free account

**Market:** Global metrics where available; localized metrics and live SERP
analysis in United States / English.

## Executive recommendation

Keep **CodexMarathon** as the distinctive product name. Do not rename the
repository to `codex-multi-account`: that exact category is already crowded,
and the product's differentiator is safer than generic account management.

Use **Codex account switcher** as the primary discovery phrase. Position the
product around a specific outcome:

> Safely switch Codex accounts without losing the active conversation.

This wording combines the highest-value validated head term with the product's
safe-boundary switching, identity verification, and conversation-recovery
capabilities.

Google-autocomplete expansion also returned recurring variants around checking
remaining usage, dashboards and status, reset timing, reduced limits, the

monthly-volume measurement.

The exact proposed names `codex supercharged`, `codex extended`, and
`codex accounts manager` had no measurable US-English metrics in the report.
`codex extended usage` appeared as a related phrase with zero reported monthly
volume. Keep **CodexMarathon** as the brand and use the measured problem and
category phrases in page titles, headings, descriptions, and explanatory copy.

### Data limits and interpretation

* Ubersuggest global mode does not apply an English-language filter; these
  metrics are global and not language-specific.
* The original global report reached the free account's daily three-report cap
  after the three global terms above. Ubersuggest returned no SEO-difficulty
  score for them.
* The 2026-09-15 US-English report supplied localized volume and difficulty for
  the usage-limit cluster. Volumes are rounded estimates, not traffic forecasts,
  and CPC can be volatile at this scale.
* The high CPC for `codex account switcher` is a strong commercial-intent
  signal. It should be the main landing-page target.
* The next US-English research pass should measure `codex CLI multiple
  accounts`, `switch Codex accounts`, `Codex account switching`, `Codex usage
  limit account switch`, and `switch Codex account without losing
  conversation`.

* `codex account switcher windows`
* `codex account switcher mac`
* `codex account switcher vscode`
* `codex desktop account switcher`

### Terms to avoid targeting

Do not build content around terms associated with policy evasion or irrelevant
intent, including `codex multi account ban`, `codex multiple free accounts`,
Nintendo Switch queries, Roblox/executor results, and generic `codex switch`.
Use clear qualifiers such as **OpenAI Codex CLI**, **ChatGPT accounts**, and
**accounts you control** to reduce ambiguity.

## Recommended information architecture

### 1. Product landing page

**Suggested title**

```text
CodexMarathon — Safe Codex Account Switcher for the Codex CLI
```

**Suggested description**

```text
Safely switch between ChatGPT accounts in the OpenAI Codex CLI at turn
boundaries. Preserve conversations, recover after usage limits, and avoid
duplicate turns.
```

**Primary keyword:** `codex account switcher`  
**Supporting keywords:** `codex multi account switcher`, `codex CLI multi account`

The page should demonstrate the native commands, explain safe boundaries, and
make the distinction from proxy-only or manual `auth.json` swapping explicit.

### 2. Differentiator guide

**Suggested title**

```text
How to Switch Codex Accounts Without Losing Your Conversation
```

**Primary keyword:** `switch Codex account without losing conversation`  
**Supporting keywords:** `switch between Codex accounts`, `change Codex CLI account`

Explain the safe-boundary check, credential deployment, identity verification,
transition journal, and exactly-once continuation. This page targets the
product's unique strength and addresses an observed live-SERP angle.

### 3. Setup and workflow guide

**Suggested title**

```text
Use Multiple Codex Accounts Safely in the Codex CLI
```

**Primary keyword:** `codex multi account`  
**Supporting keywords:** `codex CLI multiple accounts`, `switch account Codex CLI`

Provide installation, login, import, manual switching, safe operational
boundaries, and a troubleshooting section.

### 4. Technical comparison page

**Suggested title**

```text
CodexMarathon vs Codex Multi-Auth: Safe Switching and Request Rotation
```

Use a factual comparison of the two approaches: native safe-boundary
transitions and recovery versus wrapper/proxy request rotation. Avoid
unsubstantiated superiority claims.

### 5. FAQ

Include concise, internally linked answers for:

* How do I switch Codex accounts?
* Can I use multiple Codex accounts in the CLI?
* How do I change the active Codex CLI account?
* Can I keep my conversation when switching accounts?
* What happens when an account reaches a usage limit?

## On-page SEO rules

* Use one descriptive H1 per page and place the target phrase naturally in the
  title, H1, opening paragraph, and relevant internal-link anchor text.
* Keep **CodexMarathon** visible as the brand, but pair it with
  **Codex account switcher** in the home-page title and description.
* Prefer original technical evidence: command examples, state-transition
  diagrams, test coverage, recovery semantics, and supported-platform details.
* Do not repeat keyword variants unnaturally. Specific, useful content and
  clear intent are more valuable than keyword density.
* Describe use as authorized management of accounts the operator controls;
  avoid positioning around quota circumvention.
